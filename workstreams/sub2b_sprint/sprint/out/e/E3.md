# E3 — One Parakeet SM8850 prebuilt

## Verdict

**Programme fork: EXPORT. Artifact-level fork: REPACKAGE only with exact QNN 2.40.**

The smallest useful SM8850 Parakeet v3 archive is real, correctly targeted, and intact. However, its encoder, decoder, and joiner contexts all embed `v2.40.0.251030114326_189385`. Lane E pins the programme bundle to QAIRT `2.45.0.260326154327`, so these particular contexts are quarantined from NPU execution and Parakeet must be recompiled at the chosen pin before the complete bundle can use it. Pairing them with 2.45 or E1's 2.48 probe runtime is forbidden.

This distinction matters: a programme choosing an isolated, exact 2.40 runtime could repackage this asset in days. The selected single-version programme cannot.
