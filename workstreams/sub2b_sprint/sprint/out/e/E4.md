## TTS search check

Scanning every model manifest in the complete pinned tree found exactly six published `Audio Generation` TTS models: `pipertts_en`, `pipertts_de`, `pipertts_it`, `melotts_en`, `melotts_es`, and `melotts_zh`. No additional likely TTS successor ID was found by manifest text or model-path search.

The requested checks are decisive:

- `pipertts_en`: 102/102 profile rows passed; all 102 name NPU and all layers are on NPU. Its Samsung Galaxy S26 encoder row is 17.027 ms **VENDOR**, 401/401 NPU layers.
- `melotts_es`: 70/70 passed and all-NPU. Its S26 encoder row is 23.829 ms **VENDOR**, 830/830 NPU layers.
- `melotts_zh`: 56/56 passed and all-NPU. Its S26 encoder row is 23.812 ms **VENDOR**, 830/830 NPU layers.
- The other three TTS IDs have the same qualitative result. These are component profiles, not end-to-end synthesis times; do not sum them.

## Non-additivity and version safety

ASR/TTS rows use `Samsung Galaxy S26`; the cited LLM row uses `Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 QRD`. They are different device entries and different workload units, so they **must not be summed** into one latency or throughput budget.

> **Runtime stop condition:** every Zipformer, Voice AI TTS, and Qwen3-0.6B QAIRT asset found here records QAIRT `2.45.0.260326154327`. E1's trivial probe used `2.48.0.260626120635`, but that context was not downloaded. Never pair these 2.45 artifacts with a 2.48 runtime.
