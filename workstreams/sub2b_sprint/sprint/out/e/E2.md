## Precision and runtime caveats

`precision` in `E2.json` is parsed from the publication convention: an `-int8.tar.bz2` suffix is `int8`; no suffix is recorded as `float`. The suffix observation is `EXACT`, but the no-suffix graph dtype remains `VENDOR` because archive payloads were intentionally not inspected. Also, “Chinese-only” must mean “only Chinese-associated offline families receive the suffix”: SenseVoice itself is multilingual (`zh-en-ja-ko-yue`).

One streaming Zipformer filename says `chunk-size-32` without a time unit. It is retained as untyped 32 and is **not** called 32 ms.

**Runtime pairing warning:** only the `asr-models-qnn-binary` release body explicitly states QNN SDK **2.40.0.251030**. The `-2` and `-3` release bodies do not explicitly pin a runtime version. E2 therefore does **not** authorize pairing those contexts with any runtime; the exact version must be established from the selected archive before use.
