# B1 — frozen five-language screen protocol

**Status:** complete. `b1_prereg.json` was frozen before any candidate-model inference.

- Preregistration SHA-256: `6107258882449913b9136ad9eba930e88952192b6df08a301eafc00f03f6255b`
- Arms: Qwen3.5-0.8B Q4_0, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M, Qwen3-0.6B Q4_0, and Falcon-H1-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M. Every base revision, quant repository revision, GGUF filename, byte count, and LFS SHA-256 is pinned.
- Per language: 80 paired Global-MMLU FULL test items plus 40 target-language generation items, for **n=120**. Global-MMLU-Lite is prohibited because it has no Russian.
- MCQ scoring: deterministic length-normalized log-likelihood over answer letters. Generation: greedy, `temperature=0`, `top_p=1`, seed 1729, maximum 192 new tokens.
- Qwen thinking is disabled at chat-template render with `enable_thinking=false`; a prompt asking the model not to reason is not accepted as a substitute.
- Headline language score: 80 MCQ correctness points plus 40 correctness-gated generation scores. Generation weights are 70% correctness, 20% mechanical surface-language/script fluency proxy, and 10% concision. Component results remain separate.
- Decision: Qwen3.5-0.8B must reach at least 0.75× its own English score in ES, FR, and RU. RU-only failure retains sub-1B with reduced scope. Any ES/FR failure, or two or more failures, moves the fork to Q/F.

This is a `HOST` screen once executed, not a native-speaker verdict or the parent plan's n≥1,200 non-inferiority study. The native prompts were authored directly in each language by Codex before outputs existed, but they were not human-native-authored or validated. The reported “fluency” metric is therefore a reproducible surface proxy, never a claim of native naturalness.
