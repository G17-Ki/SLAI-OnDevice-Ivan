# Demo requirements

This public summary preserves the acceptance requirements used by the overnight
phone-demo work without publishing the internal orchestration brief.

## Demo 1 — “IT FITS”

- Strictly fewer than 2,000,000,000 parameters.
- English, Mandarin Chinese, Spanish, French, and Russian.
- Offline execution on the phone.

## Demo 2 — “IT'S GOOD”

- Strictly fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters.
- English and Mandarin Chinese.
- Best available ASR/TTS quality within the evaluated stack.
- First in-app audio-start proxy below 3,000 ms in both languages.

Licence observations are recorded as research metadata but were not acceptance
gates for these demos. Runtime and accelerator claims remain limited to the
version-matched evidence cited in the report and evidence index.
