## How the verdicts were assigned

Applying the lane brief's rule — *"any stack with an UNKNOWN row that could cross 2 B is
UNVERIFIED, not COMPLIANT"* — with the threshold made explicit rather than left to
judgement:

- **COMPLIANT** — no `UNKNOWN` row remains; every row `EXACT`; worst case also under cap.
- **UNVERIFIED** — an `UNKNOWN` row exists that could cross the cap.
- **NONCOMPLIANT** — the measured total already exceeds the cap.

The `UNKNOWN` rows are all the same thing: **ASR-side punctuation and ITN**. A5 sized that
hazard against real measured artefacts, so the test is arithmetic rather than rhetorical.
The cheapest five-language neural punctuation restorer I could find and parse is
**58,199,117** (`1-800-BAD-CODE/punct_cap_seg_47_language`, `EXACT`).

only configuration this lane can certify is the one that was never a product candidate.

## What moved, and why

The prior ledger was good. Every stack moved by under 0.8%, and D by 8,152 parameters out
of 1.83 billion. What this lane changed is **confidence**, not arithmetic:

**Rows upgraded to `EXACT`** — every `UB` row in the programme is gone. Notably
`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` and `Falcon-H1-1.5B-Instruct` both moved `HIGH → EXACT`,
discharging **TH-61**.

**Four doubling hazards disproven, not bounded.** Because the parse enumerates dtype
rather than assuming it, the §2.5 register's fp16 scenarios cannot occur: GigaAM (all
float32), Supertonic (FLOAT), silero-vad (FLOAT), Piper (FLOAT — **TH-59 discharged**,
the one hazard that would have breached Q and F).

**Three bounds were breached** — small, but each is the mechanism by which a "conservative"
ledger stops being conservative: k2fsa-zipformer +3,873,962, canary +1,743,137, RU
Zipformer +610,041.

**One unbudgeted dependency found**: Magpie's audio codec, +62,600,978 to Stack L.

**One genuinely unpublished row closed**: silero `v5_cis_base` = 22,850,584, **77,149,416
below** its bound — the largest single correction in the lane.

## Load-bearing caveats

- **Counting convention.** `params` is *all* tensor elements (gross, conservative,
  matching §2.0); `params_learned` excludes I64/I32 index and counter buffers per §1.2.
  Totals use the conservative figure. Conservative counting can never turn a NONCOMPLIANT
  stack into a COMPLIANT one. The two bases differ by at most a few thousand parameters
  per stack.
- **Compliance ≠ shippable.** Four of six stacks carry a 🔴 licence flag (A6). The lane
  can say COMPLIANT about arithmetic; it cannot say shippable.
- **Artefact identity.** Rows are measured against the artefact I could reach. Where that
  is not provably the shipped binary — the in-project RU Zipformer, the SenseVoice QNN
  context — the row names the substitution.
- **Export-time re-materialisation.** A1's TIED verdict is about the upstream checkpoint.
  MOSS's ONNX proves exports *can* duplicate tied weights (3.3× there). Under S-2 the
  ledger counts the unique basis and carries the serialized figure as `ub_worst_case`.
