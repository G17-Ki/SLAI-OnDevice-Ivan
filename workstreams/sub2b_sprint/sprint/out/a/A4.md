## What actually threatens Q and F

`facebook/omniASR-CTC-300M`'s tokenizer is **character-level and contains zero
punctuation tokens.**

I byte-scanned the whole 87,562 B sentencepiece blob: **0 occurrences** of `.` `,` `?`
`!` `;` `:`. Digits are present; so are CJK, Cyrillic, Greek and Khmer characters — so
the scan is sound, not a decode failure. The pieces are single Unicode characters.

This is architectural evidence, not a vendor claim. §2.4a said a punctuation row for Q is
"*more* likely to be non-zero than for D". It is stronger than that: **a punctuation and
ITN source is required.** OmniASR cannot emit one.

The cheapest five-language neural punctuation restorer I could find and measure:

| repo | params | class |
|---|---:|---|
| `1-800-BAD-CODE/punct_cap_seg_47_language` | **58,199,117** | `EXACT` |
| `kredor/punctuate-all` | 277,458,184 | `EXACT` |
| `1-800-BAD-CODE/xlm-roberta_punctuation_fullstop_truecase` | 278,052,454 | `EXACT` |
| `oliverguhr/fullstop-punctuation-multilang-large` | 558,847,496 | `EXACT` |

```
Q headroom  35,517,077  −  58,199,117  =  BREACH by 22,682,040
F headroom  24,367,893  −  58,199,117  =  BREACH by 33,831,224
```

**Neither Q nor F can afford the cheapest neural punctuation restorer that exists.**

They remain under cap only if punctuation and ITN are delegated to the backbone LLM — a
zero-parameter path, since the backbone is already in the ledger and already generating
text. That is a **binding architectural constraint**, and it belongs in the build-time
assertion, not in anyone's head. This is why the verdict is **UNVERIFIED**, not
COMPLIANT: the arithmetic holds under one specific design choice that has not been made
in writing.

## The g2pW hazard, now exactly sized

§3.10 prices it as "~102,000,000". g2pW is `bert-base-chinese` plus a polyphone head, and
I parsed `google-bert/bert-base-chinese`: **102,882,442** `EXACT` — the gross figure the
plan quotes, confirmed to the parameter.

Q's headroom is 34.5% of it. F's is 23.7%.

Neither stack should ever take it, and neither needs to: rows Q7/F7 already specify
**pypinyin**, rule-based, 0 parameters. g2pW is an upgrade path, not a requirement. Worth
saying plainly because "the g2pW hazard" has been carried as a threat to Q and F when it
is really a threat only to a decision nobody has to make.
